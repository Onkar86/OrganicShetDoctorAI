🎯 WHAT THIS APP DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INPUT:     Farmer selects crop problem
              ↓
BACKEND:   Database searches for solutions
              ↓
DATABASE:  Finds matching organic remedies
              ↓
OUTPUT:    Farmer sees step-by-step instructions


EXAMPLE WORKFLOW:
1. Farmer sees: "पिवळसर पाने" (Yellow Leaves)
2. Farmer selects problem from dropdown
3. Farmer clicks "उपाय पहा" (Show Solution)
4. System shows:
   - Symptoms description
   - Root cause
   - Solution: नीम पाण्याचा फवारणी (Neem water spray)
   - Ingredients: 1 tablespoon neem powder, 1 liter water
   - Preparation: Soak neem powder 10 hours
   - Dosage: Spray every 7 days
   - Timing: Early morning or evening
   - Warning: Don't apply in hot sun
